# Widgets v2 — treatment vs baseline

On all 400 items of widgets-v2, the treatment reaches 97.50% accuracy against a
baseline of 81.25%, a gain of 6.25 points (350 correct against 325). An exact
McNemar test on the paired outcomes gives p = 0.0412.

Shape is where the treatment pays: it rises to 74.00%, the largest movement of
any category.

Head removal accounts for between two thirds and four fifths of the effect.
