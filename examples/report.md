# Widgets v2 — treatment vs baseline

On all 400 items of widgets-v2, the treatment reaches 87.50% accuracy against a
baseline of 81.25%, a gain of 6.25 points (350 correct against 325). An exact
McNemar test on the paired outcomes gives p = 0.0412.

The gain is not spread evenly across categories. Colour rises from 86.00% to
91.00% and count from 81.00% to 89.00%; shape does not move at all, staying at
74.00% under both.

Head removal accounts for between 64.43% and 81.17% of the effect.
